About the job

Lattice Pay is building the payments backbone for modern fintech. We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to own our ledger and settlement services as we scale across new markets.

What you'll do: design and operate high-throughput services in Go and Rust; partner with product on new payment rails; and raise the bar on reliability, observability, and incident response.

What we're looking for: 6+ years building backend systems at scale; deep knowledge of distributed systems and databases; and strong written communication. Mandarin or Cantonese is a nice-to-have for our APAC expansion, but is not required for this role.

Lattice Pay is a remote-first company. We offer competitive compensation, equity, and a genuine commitment to sustainable pace.
